# Changelog

## 1.2.9

Applies to:

- `firstaid-1.2.9+fabric1.21.1`
- `firstaid-1.2.9+fabric26.1`
- `firstaid-1.2.9+fabric26.2`
- `firstaid-1.2.9+neoforge1.21.1`
- `firstaid-1.2.9+neoforge26.1`
- `firstaid-1.2.9+neoforge26.2`
- `firstaid-1.2.9+forge1.20.1`

### Fixed

- Fixed body-part health being clamped to the unscaled config maximum when reloading a world after other mods raised the player's max health (`scaleMaxHealth`). Saved limb max health is restored before current health on load, and client sync now deserializes before re-running scale logic. (GitHub #5)
- Fixed downed/unconscious players still being able to use mobility abilities such as ParCool. Unconscious players now cancel ParCool TryToStart/TryToContinue via soft-compat, clear movement and ParCool keybinds on the client, and zero horizontal/upward velocity on both pre/post ticks. (GitHub #4)
- Restored the body-model HUD to render from `textures/gui/simple_health.png` on modern loaders so resource packs can retexture the overlay again. (GitHub #3)
- Fixed `LIMITED` / `LIMITED2` natural regen faking vanilla health as full once body parts hit the natural-regen cap (default 85%). That made Regeneration potions stop applying while true limb health stayed around e.g. 34/40. Limited mode now only caps food natural regeneration; potions and other external heals can restore to full again.
