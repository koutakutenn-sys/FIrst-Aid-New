# First Aid New 1.2.10

## Added
- Critical crawl: while critically downed, the first 60% of the death timer allows slow crawling. Jumping, sprinting, attacking, and interactions remain blocked. The final 40% fully locks movement again. Stabilizing after rescue never allows crawl.
- Morphine Injector (`firstaid:morphine_injector`): two-use syringe modeled after the adrenaline injector. Instantly applies 2.5× morphine pain-relief duration, forces maximum suppression when suppression is enabled, grants Regeneration I for 3 minutes, and applies the full adrenaline combat package.
- Opioid addiction system: oral morphine and the morphine injector accumulate a hidden addiction value (injection faster than oral; recreational use gains more than medical use). Stopping opioids for a while slowly decays addiction.
- Addiction withdrawal episodes: random timed episodes after addiction reaches the mild threshold. **Pain always triggers**; additional symptoms (darkness, nausea, weakness, slowness) mix, and higher addiction stacks more symptoms at once. Probability, check frequency, duration, and cooldowns all scale strongly with addiction.
- Custom morphine effect reintroduced: only while morphine is active are withdrawal episodes blocked (painkillers alone do not). Morphine duration is floating, always ≤ painkiller duration, and shorter as addiction rises.
- Milk cannot clear addiction rise/fall status effects or active withdrawal symptoms (custom milk-immune effects are reapplied from model state every tick).
- `/firstaid addiction` for players to query their own addiction value; operators can query or set another player's value (`/firstaid addiction <player>`, `/firstaid addiction set <player> <value>`).
- Status HUD pulses for addiction increase / ultra increase / decrease and multi-line withdrawal labels.

## Changed
- Addiction rise/ultra-rise status icons last 15 seconds; the addiction value itself still increases instantly on use.
- Addiction decay is slower overall (2/5 of the previous rate). Below addiction 30, decay slows further to 1/10 of the previous baseline rate.
- Oral morphine applies the custom morphine effect plus painkiller + Regeneration I (25s). Morphine duration is addiction-scaled and capped by painkiller duration.
- Morphine pill use sound plays once on start (no longer loops/repeats mid-use).
- Withdrawal is gated only by the morphine effect, not by painkillers alone.

## Config
- `criticalCrawlEnabled` (default true)
- `addictionEnabled` (default true)
- `morphineInjectorUseDuration` (default 40 ticks)
