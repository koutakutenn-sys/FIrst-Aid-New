# First Aid New 1.3.1

*Pain that spikes, settles, and still has somewhere left to go.*

Applies to:

- `firstaid-1.3.1+fabric1.21.1`
- `firstaid-1.3.1+fabric26.2`
- `firstaid-1.3.1+neoforge1.21.1`
- `firstaid-1.3.1+neoforge26.2`
- `firstaid-1.3.1+forge1.20.1`

## Highlights

1.3.0 graded the screen; 1.3.1 grades **time**. A nick no longer spends the whole pain budget, and a hard hit can still white the world out for a breath before the nervous system remembers how to breathe.

## Pain system

### Chronic vs acute

Pain is no longer a single static integer from injury alone.

| Layer | Source | Behaviour |
|-------|--------|-----------|
| **Chronic** | Visible body-part injury | Softer low-end curve; stays until you heal |
| **Acute** | Recent damage amount | Spikes on hit, decays over a few seconds |
| **Display level** (Mild→Critical) | Combined | Still 1–5 for HUD / API |

- **Higher visual ceiling** (`PAIN_HARD_CAP` ≈ 1.85): brief near-blind blur / FOV tunnel after heavy trauma, then accelerated decay so white-out never sticks.
- **Wider mild/moderate bands**: a few drops of health no longer slam you into extreme/critical presentation.
- **~25% acute breakthrough** under morphine/painkillers: opioids mute the wound, not the impact language of a hard hit.
- Optional **sprint flare** when already severely injured, and a short **hitch of slowness** when acute pain crosses a high threshold (cooldown-limited).

### Client feedback

- Blur and FOV follow continuous intensity (can exceed 1.0 during spikes).
- Severe tinnitus also fires on a large **acute** edge, not only on discrete level ≥4.
- Smoother approach rates: fast rise on spike, slower settle.

## Morphine

### Rush window (first 10 seconds)

When morphine **activates** (oral after delay, or injector immediately), a **10s rush** plays layered random stingers from a fixed pool (heartbeat, soft tinnitus, beacon wash, respawn-anchor charge, amethyst chime, etc.). Sounds may overlap; volumes stay moderate.

### Saturation curve

Morphine colour grade no longer tracks remaining time with a simple smoothstep of `ticksLeft/max`.

It now uses a **decelerating exponential** fade over the dose (`exp(-k · progress)`), with a clean end fade and a **high floor during the 10s rush** so onset always reads vivid.

## Technical

- New model fields synced via existing damage-model NBT: `acutePainIntensity`, `chronicPainIntensity`, `morphineRushTicks`.
- `PlayerDamageModel.getPainVisualStrength(boolean suppressed)`, `getMorphineSaturationFactor()`, `getMorphineRushTicks()`.
- Client: `MorphineRushController` on all five loaders.

## Closing

1.3.1 is the release that gives pain a **before and after**: the hit, the gasp, and the long ache you still have to treat.
